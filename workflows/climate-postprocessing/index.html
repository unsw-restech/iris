Climate

Climate Data Post-processing

Regridding, temporal aggregation and climate index calculation over NetCDF archives using CDO and NCO. Handles the routine reshaping work between raw model output and analysis — remapping to a common grid, computing monthly and seasonal means, and deriving standard indices.

▶  Run on Katana Opens Katana OnDemand — log in with your zID when prompted.
Version
v2.1.0
Engine
CDO + NCO
Compute
Katana
CPUs (default)
8

What to expect

  1. Click Run on Katana and log in with your zID.
  2. Point input_dir at your NetCDF files.
  3. Choose the target grid to remap onto, or keep the native grid.
  4. Select the aggregation — monthly, seasonal or annual means.
  5. Set your output directory to your scratch space.
  6. Adjust walltime and resources if needed, then click Submit.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultDescription
input_dir—Directory of NetCDF input files
variabletasVariable to process — e.g. tas, pr, psl
target_gridnativeGrid to remap onto, or native to skip remapping
aggregationmonthlyTemporal aggregation — daily, monthly, seasonal, annual
indicesnoneClimate indices to derive — e.g. tx90p, rx5day
outdir—Output directory — use your scratch space
ncpus4Number of CPU cores
walltime06:00Maximum walltime (hours:minutes)

Outputs

OutputDescription
*_remapped.ncInput regridded onto the target grid
*_monthly.ncTemporally aggregated fields
indices.ncDerived climate indices, when requested
cdo.logFull CDO and NCO operator log

Environment

Runs on Katana with module load cdo/2.1.0. Nothing is installed into your home directory.
