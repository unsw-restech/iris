Molecular Dynamics (GROMACS)

Biomolecular molecular dynamics with GPU acceleration — energy minimisation, equilibration and production runs from a prepared structure and topology. Suited to protein stability, ligand binding and membrane simulations.

▶  Run on Katana Opens Katana OnDemand — log in with your zID when prompted.
Version
v2023.5
Engine
GROMACS
Compute
Katana GPU
GPU (default)
1x

What to expect

  1. Click Run on Katana and log in with your zID.
  2. Point structure and topology at your prepared system.
  3. Choose the run stage — minimisation, equilibration or production.
  4. Set the simulation length and output frequency.
  5. Set your output directory to your scratch space.
  6. Adjust walltime and resources if needed, then click Submit.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultDescription
structure—Input coordinates (.gro or .pdb)
topology—System topology (.top) with force field parameters
mdp—Run control file (.mdp) defining the integrator and conditions
stageproductionRun stage — minim, nvt, npt, production
nsteps500000Number of integration steps
outdir—Output directory — use your scratch space
ncpus8Number of CPU cores
ngpus1Number of GPUs — GROMACS offloads non-bonded terms
walltime24:00Maximum walltime (hours:minutes)

Outputs

OutputDescription
traj.xtcCompressed trajectory for analysis
confout.groFinal coordinates, for continuing the run
ener.edrEnergies, temperature and pressure over time
md.logRun log including performance in ns/day

Environment

Runs on Katana with module load gromacs/2023.5-py3.10. Nothing is installed into your home directory.
