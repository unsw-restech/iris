Hello World

A minimal test job to confirm your IRIS → Katana setup is working end-to-end. No input data required. Completes in under 5 minutes.

▶  Run on Katana

Opens Katana OnDemand — you will be prompted to log in with your zID.

Walltime
< 5 min
Input data
None needed

What happens when you click Run

  1. Your browser opens the IRIS job form on Katana OnDemand.
  2. Log in with your zID and password if prompted.
  3. Review the pre-filled parameters — no changes needed for a basic test.
  4. Click Submit in the OpenComposer form.
  5. The job runs on a Katana compute node and writes result.txt and commit.txt (the IRIS commit that ran) to your output directory.
