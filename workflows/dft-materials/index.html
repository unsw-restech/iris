Materials DFT (Quantum ESPRESSO)

Plane-wave density functional theory for periodic solids — electronic structure, total energies, forces and stress. Ships a bulk silicon calculation that converges in seconds, so you can confirm the whole chain works before pointing it at your own structure, cutoffs and k-point mesh. For isolated molecules rather than crystals, see the ORCA workflow under Chemistry.

▶  Run on Katana Opens Katana OnDemand — log in with your zID when prompted.

What to expect

  1. Click Run on Katana and log in with your zID.
  2. No input data is needed — a bulk silicon SCF calculation is included, and its 74 KB pseudopotential downloads automatically.
  3. Point input at your own Quantum ESPRESSO input file when you're ready, and put its pseudopotentials in run/pseudo/.
  4. Set your output directory to your scratch space.
  5. Raise ncpus for larger cells — pw.x runs under MPI when more than one core is requested.
  6. Set walltime to suit the system size, then click Submit.

Parameters

Outputs

*.logJob log, with the converged total energy and Fermi level extracted

Run it without OnDemand

The job script is in the repository and can be submitted directly from a Katana login node. The environment is module load quantum-espresso; nothing is installed into your account.

cd /srv/scratch/$USER
curl -O https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PatrickTung/ares/main/workflows/dft-materials/katana/ares_dft_materials.pbs

qsub ares_dft_materials.pbs            # fetches si_scf.in and the pseudopotential
