Astronomy Data Reduction

Reduction of ESO instrument data with EsoReflex — bias and flat correction, wavelength calibration, extraction and quality control. Runs the standard ESO pipelines headless so whole observing runs can be reduced in one submission.

▶  Run on Katana Opens Katana OnDemand — log in with your zID when prompted.
Version
v2023.03
Engine
EsoReflex
Compute
Katana
CPUs (default)
8

What to expect

  1. Click Run on Katana and log in with your zID.
  2. Point raw_dir at your raw ESO frames and calibrations.
  3. Select the instrument pipeline matching your data.
  4. Set the calibration database location, or let the job resolve it.
  5. Set your output directory to your scratch space.
  6. Adjust walltime and resources if needed, then click Submit.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultDescription
raw_dir—Directory of raw ESO FITS frames
instrument—ESO instrument pipeline — e.g. XSHOOTER, MUSE, UVES
calib_dirautoStatic calibration data; resolved automatically when unset
outdir—Output directory — use your scratch space
ncpus8Number of CPU cores
mem32gbMemory allocation — raise it for integral field data
walltime12:00Maximum walltime (hours:minutes)

Environment

Runs on Katana with module load esoreflex/20230313. Nothing is installed into your home directory.
