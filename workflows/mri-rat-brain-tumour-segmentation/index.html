Rat Brain Tumour Segmentation

Segment tumours in rat brain MRI volumes and report the tumour volume of each, so longitudinal preclinical studies can follow tumour growth across time points.

Planned. This workflow has no form yet, so it cannot be run. This page describes what it will do.

What it will take and produce

Description
InputRat brain MRI volumes, staged in your scratch.
OutputA tumour mask for each volume and a table of tumour volumes, with the run recorded.
