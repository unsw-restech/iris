microCT Reconstruction

Reconstruct microCT volumes from their raw projections on Katana, including sparse-view or limited-angle acquisitions that standard reconstruction handles poorly.

Planned. This workflow has no form yet, so it cannot be run. This page describes what it will do.

What it will take and produce

Description
InputThe raw projections of an acquisition, staged in your scratch.
OutputThe reconstructed volume, ready for the other microCT workflows, with the run recorded.
