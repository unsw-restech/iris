Diffusion MRI (QSIPrep)

Diffusion-weighted MRI preprocessing and reconstruction — denoising, distortion and eddy-current correction, coregistration, then orientation distribution functions and tractography via MRtrix3.

▶  Run on Katana Opens Katana OnDemand — log in with your zID when prompted.
Version
v1.0.1
Engine
QSIPrep + MRtrix3
Compute
Katana GPU
GPU (default)
1x

What to expect

  1. Click Run on Katana and log in with your zID.
  2. Point bids_dir at your BIDS dataset containing DWI series.
  3. Select the participants to process.
  4. Choose the reconstruction workflow — for example mrtrix_multishell_msmt.
  5. Set your output directory to your scratch space.
  6. Adjust walltime and resources if needed, then click Submit.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultDescription

recon_specmrtrix_multishell_msmtReconstruction workflow to apply after preprocessing
output_resolution1.2Isotropic output resolution in mm
outdir—Output directory — use your scratch space
ncpus16Number of CPU cores
ngpus1Number of GPUs — accelerates eddy-current correction
walltime48:00Maximum walltime — allow several hours per subject

Outputs

OutputDescription
sub-*/dwi/Preprocessed diffusion series with corrected gradients
*_desc-preproc_dwi.nii.gzDenoised, distortion-corrected DWI

Environment

Runs on Katana with module load neurodesk/qsiprep/1.0.1. Nothing is installed into your home directory. Neurodesk provides the tool as a container image managed for you.
