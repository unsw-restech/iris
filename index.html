IRIS is UNSW's AI imaging gateway on the Katana supercomputer. Choose your lab's workflow, transfer your dataset once, and run AI analysis on Katana's GPUs from a web form: no terminal, no software to install. Every run is recorded so you can compare experiments, and workflows are minted with a DOI you can cite.

Partner with us

IRIS is built for UNSW's imaging labs and facilities. If your research needs more than a ready-made workflow, please get in touch: ResTech works with labs on research collaborations in imaging and AI methods, supports grant applications with letters of support and in-kind contributions, and builds bespoke workflows for analyses too complex for a self-serve form.
