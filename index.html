How it works Four steps to running a workflow
01 / STAGE
Stage your dataset
Copy your dataset to your Katana scratch once, with rsync or WinSCP. How to stage a dataset
02 / RUN
Launch in two clicks
Click Run on Katana on the workflow page, then submit the form.
03 / VIEW & COMPARE
Check and compare runs
In a Katana Virtual Desktop, view results in napari and compare runs in MLflow with iris-compare. How to view and compare
04 / CITE
Cite in your paper
Cite IRIS with the workflow's name and the commit in your run's commit.txt, and acknowledge Katana. Citable DOIs through WorkflowHub are planned. How to cite
